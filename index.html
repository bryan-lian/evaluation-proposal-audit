Evaluation Proposal Audit

A checklist for the AI-Assisted Evaluation Proposal Critique. Read the proposal yourself first, one pass at a time. Then log your AI prompt, pick four flaws the AI raised, and test each one against your own audit and the course readings.

This is an individual assignment. Do your own audit and AI critique before talking with anyone. If you share this tool, share the blank file, not your filled-in work.

  1. Step 1. Audit the proposal yourself. Work through Passes 1 to 5 before you open an AI tool. Mark each line Strength, Gap, Flag, or N/A, and note where you saw it (section or page).
  2. Step 2. Prompt the AI. Log the platform, your exact prompt, and any follow-ups that changed the output. Save the full chat for your appendix.
  3. Step 3. Test four AI flaws. Summarize each, say agree, partially agree, or disagree, back it up with the proposal and readings, and say why it matters.
  4. Step 4. Reflect. What the AI did well, where it missed, and how your prompt shaped it. Then copy, download, or print the summary.

How to mark each line

StrengthAddressed, and done well. The assignment asks for strengths too, so note why.
GapMissing. The proposal never takes it up.
FlagPresent but wrong, unsupported, or contradicts itself. These are your strongest findings.
N/ADoes not apply to this proposal. Say why in the note.
