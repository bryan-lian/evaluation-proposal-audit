Evaluation Proposal Audit

Five reading passes over an evaluation proposal. Run one pass at a time rather than hunting for problems all at once, and you are checking a document against a list instead of critiquing it cold.

The prompts describe what to look for. They do not assume what you will find. A well-built proposal should come back mostly marked Present.

Passes

How to mark each line

PresentAddressed, and adequately. 1
GapMissing. The proposal never takes it up. 2
FlagPresent but wrong, unsupported, or contradicting something elsewhere. 3
N/ADoes not apply to this proposal. Kept out of the findings. 4
Click a prompt to select it, then use the number keys. N jumps to its note, J and K move between prompts.

Findings

Everything marked Gap or Flag, with your notes. Quote the page and the text so each finding can be checked against the document.

Passes follow the CDC program evaluation framework steps and the Joint Committee standards. Your marks stay in this browser. Save to a file to keep an audit, move it to another machine, or hand it to a colleague for comparison.
