# Evaluation Proposal Audit

A self-contained tool for evaluation students to audit an evaluation proposal and test an AI-generated critique of it. Built for the AI-Assisted Evaluation Proposal Critique in Evaluation Procedures.

## How to use
1. Open the link. It runs in your browser and works offline. Nothing is sent anywhere.
2. Work through the four steps: your own audit (five passes), your AI prompt log, four AI flaws tested against your audit, and a reflection.
3. Your work saves automatically in your browser on that device.
4. Use "Save to file (.json)" to back up your work or move it to another computer, and "Open saved file" to load it again.
5. Use "Copy summary", "Download as text", or "Print or save as PDF" to bring your findings into your draft.
6. "Load worked example" shows a filled-in sample based on a made-up proposal.

## Sharing
This is an individual assignment. Share the blank tool link, not your filled-in work or your saved .json file.
